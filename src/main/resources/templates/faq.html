Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before your trip.

Do I need to book tickets in advance?

Advance bookings are recommended during weekends and holidays to avoid waiting at the gate.

Is the park suitable for children?

Yes. We have dedicated family zones, child-friendly rides and guided areas for younger guests.

Are there locker facilities?

Secure lockers are available for visitors and are ideal for water park and ride day access.

What is your refund policy?

Tickets can be cancelled based on the selected policy. Review the cancellation terms before booking.
