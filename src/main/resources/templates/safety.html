Guest Safety Park Guidelines
Priority Safety

Safe Fun

Safety is part of the experience.

We take guest wellbeing seriously with clear rules, trained staff, accessible support and monitored ride safety procedures.

Ride Safety

  • Follow height and age restrictions.
  • Listen carefully to staff instructions.
  • Safely secure loose items before boarding.

Water Safety

  • Swim within designated areas only.
  • Use life jackets as advised.
  • Monitor children at all times.

Guest Support

  • First aid desks are available throughout the park.
  • Accessibility support can be requested at guest services.
  • Security and support teams are present throughout public areas.

General Policies

  • Children must be supervised by adults.
  • Prohibited items may be stored at guest services.
  • Always follow posted rules for a safe day.
