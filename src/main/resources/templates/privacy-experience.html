This policy explains what information we collect when you plan a park visit, reserve a stay, or contact guest services.

PRIVACY POLICYLast updated: 4 October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you make a reservation, we collect the details needed to manage it: your name, email address, optional phone number, visit or check-in date, guest count, selected pass or room, and the reservation reference. If you contact us, we also receive the information and message you choose to provide.

2. How we use it

We use this information to create and retrieve reservations, provide guest support, confirm the details of a visit, manage cancellations, and maintain the safety and operation of Adventure World. We do not use booking details for unrelated marketing unless you separately choose to receive it.

3. Payments

When online payment is offered, payment details are handled by the payment provider shown at checkout. Adventure World does not store full card numbers or card security codes. Reservation and payment references may be retained to reconcile your booking and help resolve payment questions.

4. When information is shared

We share only the information needed with service providers who support hosting, reservation processing, payment processing, and guest communications. Those providers may use the information only to perform the services they provide to Adventure World. We do not sell guest personal information.

5. Retention and security

We retain reservation and support information for as long as needed to provide the requested service, resolve disputes, and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, while recognizing that no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may use your booking reference and booking email to look up or cancel an eligible reservation. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal information, contact hello@adventureworld.in. We may need to verify your identity before making changes.

7. Children and cookies

Reservations should be submitted by an adult on behalf of their visiting group; do not submit unnecessary information about children. The site uses essential browser storage for features such as saving an itinerary. You can clear that local storage through your browser settings.

8. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The current version and its update date will appear on this page. Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@adventureworld.in.
